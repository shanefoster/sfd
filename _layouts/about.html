A Little About Me

At a glance: 15+ years in product design • 5+ years in design systems • Designer who codes • People-first collaborator

My Innie

For over 15 years, I’ve worked at the intersection of consumer and enterprise products, with the last 5+ years dedicated to building scalable design systems. I’m a technical designer who’s comfortable in the code and the "messy middle" where design meets engineering. Lately, I’ve been deeply exploring how AI can further bridge that gap and transform the way we build together.

While the craft matters, I’ve learned that adoption isn't built on tooling—it’s built on relationships. My focus is on creating shared languages that reduce friction and make collaboration feel effortless.

At the end of the day, I just want to build systems that people actually enjoy using while being the kind of low-ego, supportive partner that makes the work fun.
