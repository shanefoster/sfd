The Challenge

We are a small team working on a product scaling faster than anyone ever could have imagined. It's a highly competitive market and speed if of the essence.

There was hardly a moment to take a step back and assess the work. We knew were making an impact. We knew we were having success, but that's not enough.

It is imperative we demonstrate the value and impact of the work we do to our partners and stakeholders. At the time, it was not. That was a problem.

We need to make our impact as visible and accessible to them as possible. That was my mission.

What I Built

A lightweight analytics pipeline + dashboard

I built a React dashboard (running locally + deployable via Vercel), powered by Figma API ingestion for Library Analytics, Supabase for storing/normalizing data, and a codebase analyzer to measure engineering parity.
